Orbit Jump is a small arcade game by **Loxley Orbit**. It collects as little as
possible.

**What we collect**
- A **nickname you choose** when submitting a score (up to 20 characters; not required to be your real name).
- Your **game score** for that run.

That is the only data sent off your device, and only when you choose to submit a score.

**What we do NOT collect** — no accounts/passwords/email, no ads or ad-targeting
IDs, no location, contacts, browsing history, or files, and no sensitive or
financial data.

**Analytics** — we use **Google Analytics 4** to understand how the game is used
(page views, approximate country-level location, device/browser type, and simple
in-game events such as a round starting or ending, including the score reached).
It uses cookies / local identifiers, is processed by Google on our behalf, is
**not** used for advertising, and does not collect your name or anything that
directly identifies you. Opt out via the
[Google Analytics opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) or by
blocking cookies for this site.

**Stored on your device only** — best score, last-used nickname, and a local
cache of recent leaderboard entries live in your browser's local storage.

**Online leaderboard** — submitted nicknames + scores are stored on our
leaderboard service (Google Cloud Firestore) and shown publicly in-game.

**Third-party services** — Google Cloud (Firestore & App Engine) hosts the
leaderboard; Google Analytics 4 provides anonymous usage statistics (see
Analytics above); Google Fonts serves the typefaces (your browser requests fonts
from Google, which may receive your IP as part of that request).

**Children** — family-friendly; no knowing collection of personal data.

**Your choices** — clear browser storage to remove local data; contact us to
remove a leaderboard entry.
