Orbit Jump — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 August 2026

Orbit Jump is a small arcade game made by Loxley Orbit. We designed it to collect as little as possible. This page explains exactly what the game handles and why.

What we collect

  • A nickname you choose. When you submit a score to the online leaderboard, you type a display name (up to 20 characters). You pick it — it does not have to be your real name.
  • Your game score. The numeric score for that run.

That is the only information sent off your device, and only when you choose to submit a score.

What we do NOT collect

  • No accounts, sign-in, passwords, or email addresses.
  • No advertising, ad IDs, or third-party trackers.
  • No analytics or behavioural profiling.
  • No location, contacts, browsing history, or files.
  • No sensitive or financial information of any kind.

Stored on your device only

Your best score, your last-used nickname, and a local copy of recent leaderboard entries are saved in your browser's local storage so the game remembers them between sessions. This never leaves your device unless you submit a score. Clearing your browser data removes it.

The online leaderboard

If you submit a score, your chosen nickname and score are stored on our leaderboard service (hosted on Google Cloud Firestore) and shown publicly on the in-game leaderboard alongside other players. Do not use a nickname you would not want shown publicly. We keep leaderboard entries so the board persists over time; you can ask us to remove an entry (see Contact).

Third-party services

  • Google Cloud (Firestore & App Engine) hosts the leaderboard data and API.
  • Google Fonts is used for the game's typefaces; loading a font causes your browser to request it from Google, which may receive your IP address as part of that standard web request.

Children

Orbit Jump is family-friendly and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The only user-entered data is an optional display nickname for the leaderboard.

Data retention & your choices

Local data stays until you clear your browser storage. Leaderboard entries remain until removed. To request removal of a leaderboard entry, contact us with the nickname and approximate score/date.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
